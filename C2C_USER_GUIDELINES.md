# Connect to Campus (C2C) - User Guidelines

Welcome to the **Connect to Campus (C2C)** system by **SOFTNET Technologies**. This comprehensive user guide is designed to help Parents/Students, Teachers, and Staff Members (including Library, Hostel, and Transport staff) navigate and utilize all modules of the platform.

---

## 👨‍👩‍👦 1. Parent & Student User Guide
The Parent & Student portal offers complete access to academic progress, fees, daily schedules, leaves, and safety tracking.

### Step 1: Logging In
1. Open the application.
2. Enter your registered email (e.g., student demo: `student@demo.com`) and password (`123456`).
3. Click **Login**. 
> [!NOTE]
> The onboarding welcome page will only appear once per session to ensure a fast dashboard load.

### Step 2: Navigating the Dashboard (Overview)
Upon logging in, you will see a high-level summary:
* **Attendance**: Real-time percentage check.
* **Library**: Count of currently issued books and overdue alerts.
* **Fees**: Outstanding dues.
* **Hostel**: Room details (if allocated).
* **Transport**: Assigned route and status.
* **Notice Board**: The latest announcements from school authorities.

### Step 3: Academic Tracking & Exams
1. Click on **Academics & Exams** in the menu.
2. **Weekly Timetable**: Check class schedules.
3. **Exams**: View upcoming examination dates and locations.
4. **Marks & Report Cards**: Review term results, marks, grades, and class toppers.

### Step 4: Academic Doubts
1. Click **Ask Doubts**.
2. Select your **Subject** from the list.
3. Type your question or concern in the text box.
4. Click **Submit Doubt**. Teachers will be notified and reply directly on your dashboard.

### Step 5: Check Attendance History
1. Click **Attendance**.
2. View the calendar showing days marked **Present** (Green), **Absent** (Red), or **Leave** (Yellow).

### Step 6: Fee Management & Online Receipts
1. Click **My Fees**.
2. Review all outstanding tuition, transport, or hostel fees.
3. Click **View History** to look at past payments and download PDF copies of official receipts.

### Step 7: Applying for Leave
1. Click **Apply Leave**.
2. Select the **Leave Type** (e.g., Sick, Casual).
3. Select **Start Date**, **End Date**, and input a brief reason.
4. Click **Submit Leave Application**. 
5. Track status (Pending ➜ Approved/Rejected) on the same screen. You will receive real-time push alerts on your mobile device when updated.

### Step 8: Safety & Live Bus Tracking
1. Click **Live Fleet Map**.
2. View the real-time location of your assigned school bus to know exactly when to reach the stop.

### Step 9: Library & Hostel Portals
* **Library Books**: Check books in your possession, due dates, and borrow history.
* **Hostel Rooms**: View allocated block, room number, roommates, and pending hostel bills.

## 👨‍🏫 2. Teacher User Guide
The Teacher portal is optimized for managing student records, attendance, timetables, and doubt clearing.

### Step 1: Logging In
1. Login with your credentials (e.g., teacher demo: `teacher@demo.com` / `123456`).
2. Verify your **Subject Specialization** and **Assigned Class** on the Home screen.

### Step 2: Marking Student Attendance
Teachers have access to three methods for taking attendance:
#### Option A: Manual Entry
1. Navigate to **Mark Attendance**.
2. Select the **Class** and **Section**.
3. Toggle the status button next to each student's name: **Present**, **Absent**, or **Leave**.
4. Click **Submit Attendance**.

#### Option B: Biometric Face Enrollment (First time only)
1. Go to **Face Enrollment**.
2. Select the **Student Name** from the list.
3. Align the camera with the student's face and click **Enroll Face**.

#### Option C: Biometric Face Scanner
1. Go to **Face Scanner**.
2. Position the scanner at the door/classroom entrance.
3. Let the camera automatically recognize students and record their attendance in real-time.

### Step 3: Checking Attendance Reports
1. Go to **Daily Status** to see quick summaries of today's attendance.
2. Go to **Attendance Reports** to query class historical attendance by date range or specific student.

### Step 4: Resolving Student Doubts
1. Click **Student Doubts**.
2. View the list of active doubts asked by students in your subjects.
3. Click **Reply**, type the explanation, and click **Submit**.

### Step 5: Adding Student Reviews & Feedback
1. Click **Student Reviews**.
2. Select a student and input performance reports, behavioral remarks, or constructive comments for parents.

### Step 6: AI Question Paper Generator (For schools with Exams enabled)
1. Click **AI Question Paper**.
2. Select the subject and enter the number of questions.
3. Pick the difficulty level and topics.
4. Click **Generate** to create a formatted test paper sourced from the **NEET & JEE Master Bank**.

### Step 7: Teacher Self-Service
* **My Timetable**: Check class schedules.
* **My Salary**: View monthly pay details and breakdown.
* **Leave Applications**: Apply for personal leaves.
* **Out Pass**: Request digital out-passes to leave the campus during working hours.
* **Profile Settings**: Update profile pictures and personal info.

## 👔 3. Employee & Staff User Guide
The Staff portal supports general administrative staff, drivers, library admins, and hostel admins.

### 🚛 A. For School Bus Drivers (Trip Tracking)
Drivers must broadcast their location to parents and administration during transit.
1. Log in with your Driver credentials.
2. Go to the **Trip Tracking** tab.
3. Select your assigned **Vehicle** from the drop-down menu.
4. Click **START TRACKING**.
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Keep your mobile phone GPS enabled. The app will broadcast coordinates every 3-5 seconds.
5. Once the route is complete, click **STOP TRACKING**.
6. Review the logs in the **Activity Log** box to ensure coordinates are syncing.

### 📚 B. For Library Administrators (Librarians)
Library Admins manage book inventory and check-in/check-out logs.
1. Expand **Library Management** in the sidebar.
2. **Library Overview**: Monitor active issues, total catalog counts, and overdue notices.
3. **Manage Books**:
   * Add new titles, specify categories, authors, and shelf locations.
   * Update existing book inventory details.
4. **Circulation Desk (Issue & Return)**:
   * **Issue Book**: Enter the Student/Teacher ID, select the Book ID, set a due date, and submit.
   * **Return Book**: Search for the active loan record and click **Process Return**.

### 🏨 C. For Hostel Administrators (Wardens)
Hostel Admins handle allocations, room maintenance, and boarding fees.
1. Expand **Hostel Management** in the sidebar.
2. **Hostel Overview**: Inspect block capacities and occupancy statistics.
3. **Room Configurations**: Define blocks, room numbers, room capacities, and rent rates.
4. **Room Allocations**:
   * Allocate rooms to students.
   * Process hostel check-outs and vacate records.
5. **Hostel Finances**: Generate boarding bills, log payment receipts, and manage outstanding boarding balances.

### 💼 D. General Employee Self-Service (All Staff Members)
General staff can perform self-service operations:
* **My Attendance**: Check check-in/check-out history.
* **Salary Slips**: View and download monthly payslips.
* **Leave Applications**: Apply for leave and track Warden/HR approvals.
* **Out Pass**: Request temporary gate passes for stepping out of campus.
* **Notice Board & Calendar**: Stay updated with campus announcements.
